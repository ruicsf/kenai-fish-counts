How the timing works
Settings

Lag is hours from high tide at the mouth to fish reaching your spot. Your holes are at about river mile 47 (ADF&G puts the Kenai Keys markers at 44.6). Default 31–47 assumes 1–1.5 mph over ground across those 47 miles. The RM 19 sonar checkpoint scales with it automatically. Use the pace chips above for a starting point, then fine-tune here once you've calibrated against real fishing.

Tides: NOAA CO-OPS Kenai City Pier (predictions, MLLW) · Counts: ADF&G RM 19 DIDSON sonar · Historical baseline 2022–2025 baked in. Times use your device clock — keep it on Alaska time. Estimates only, not for navigation.
